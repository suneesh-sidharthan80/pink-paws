Why regular check-ups matter

Cats are experts at hiding when they feel unwell.

A cat can seem perfectly fine while a problem quietly grows. Regular visits to a vet are the surest way to catch it in time. The hardest part is often just getting there, and that is where we help.

  • Problems found early are easier to treat

    Teeth, kidneys, joints and weight can change slowly over months. A vet who sees your cat regularly can notice small changes before they become serious.

  • Vaccines and prevention stay on schedule

    Booster shots, deworming and flea and tick control all work best on time. We help you keep those dates without the stress of the trip.

  • A healthier cat is a happier cat

    A cat that is free of pain plays more, eats well and stays close to you. Many vets suggest a check-up at least once a year, and more often for older cats. Your vet will tell you what is right for yours.

worth a vet visit

Signs to watch for at home

  • Eating less, or eating more but losing weight
  • Drinking much more water than usual
  • Changes in litter box habits
  • Bad breath or trouble chewing
  • Hiding, sleeping more or not jumping up
  • Vomiting, or a dull and untidy coat
