Questions

Good to know.

Are you a veterinary clinic?

No. Pink Paws is an independent cat-care and travel assistance service. Diagnosis and treatment are always provided by qualified veterinary professionals of your choice.

Which hospitals and clinics do you go to?

Any veterinary hospital, clinic or grooming centre in Bengaluru that you choose. If you don't have one, we can help you arrange a visit.

Do you really work at night?

Yes, we're available 24/7, including late nights and holidays. For urgent situations, call rather than message.

Can I come along?

Of course. You're welcome to ride with us, or stay home and get updates on WhatsApp.

Do you take dogs or other pets?

No. Pink Paws is cat-only, so your cat never shares a ride or a carrier with other animals.

How much does it cost?

It depends on distance, timing and the kind of visit. Message us with the details and we'll give you a quote before anything is booked.
